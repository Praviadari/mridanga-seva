Get the app

The Mridanga Seva app is for the students and coordinators of the class. It shows your visits, your progress through the syllabus, announcements, lessons and practice tools such as a metronome.

Android phones

Install the Android app from its download page on expo.dev, the service that builds it. The page shows a QR code and an Install button.

Download for Android

The app is not in the Google Play Store yet, so Android asks you once to allow installing apps from your browser. The app then updates itself.

A Google Play Store listing will follow.

iPhone, iPad and computers

Coming soon. The web version will open at app.mridangaseva.com in any browser. On an iPhone, you can add it to your home screen from Safari (Share, then Add to Home Screen) and use it like an installed app.

Signing in

Sign up with the email address you gave at the desk (see How to join). The app is in English, Telugu and Hindi; it follows your phone's language, and you can change it in the app.

The app does not show ads and does not track you. See the privacy notice for what it keeps.
