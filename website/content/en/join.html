How to join

Sign up in the app before you come; at the class, joining then takes a minute. Under 18, or no smartphone? Just come: you are registered at the desk.

  1. Sign up in the app

    Install the app (see Get the app) and create an account: your full name as on your Aadhaar or other government ID, date of birth, gender, phone number, email address and your class centre. Tick that you have read the privacy notice. Confirm your email from the message you receive. Your sign-up then waits for the desk.

    Under 18, or no smartphone? Skip this step: you are registered at the desk (step 3).

  2. Come to the class

    Come during the open hours (see Classes) and bring your ID, so the coordinator can check your name. We keep only the name, never the ID number or a copy. If you have questions first, write to {{email:info}}.

  3. Get registered at the desk

    Tell the coordinator your name. They find your sign-up and accept it, and you get a roll number, such as MS-2026-0001, that stays yours. In the app, tap Check again: your student home opens.

    No sign-up? The coordinator registers you in about two minutes: your name, a phone number or email address, and your area or PIN code (not your full address).

    Under 18? Come with your parent or guardian. They give their name and phone number and sign a consent form. You get a printed QR card for checking in.

    The app is optional. Without it, a coordinator checks you in at the desk.

  4. Start at the Beginner level

    Learn the first strokes with a coordinator, practise at home, and come back when you can. Your progress is ticked off item by item, and the facilitator assesses you when you are ready for the next level.
