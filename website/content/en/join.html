How to join

Joining takes a few minutes at the class. You do not need to sign up anywhere before you come.

  1. Come to the class

    Come during the open hours (see Classes). If you have questions first, write to {{email:info}}.

  2. Register at the desk

    A coordinator registers you in about two minutes: your name, a phone number or email address, and your area or PIN code (not your full address). You get a roll number, such as MS-2026-0001, that stays yours.

    Under 18? Your parent or guardian gives their name and phone number and signs a consent form.

  3. Sign in to the app

    Install the app (see Get the app) and sign up with the same email address you gave at the desk. Confirm your email from the message you receive; your login is then linked to your student record.

    The app is optional. Without it, a coordinator checks you in at the desk.

  4. Start at the Beginner level

    Learn the first strokes with a coordinator, practise at home, and come back when you can. Your progress is ticked off item by item, and the facilitator assesses you when you are ready for the next level.
