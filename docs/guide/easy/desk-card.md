# Desk card — for whoever is at the desk today

Read in: **English** · [తెలుగు](te/desk-card.md) · [हिन्दी](hi/desk-card.md)

One page. Print it and keep it at the desk. Hare Krishna, and thank you for your seva. 🙏

[← Easy guide](README.md)

---

### A student arrives

1. Ask them to open **My QR card** on their phone.
2. On your phone: **Attendance** tab → **Scan a QR code** → hold their code in front of the camera.
3. You see **Checked in at …**. Done.

No phone? Under **Find by name**, type 2 letters of the name → **Check in**.

### A student leaves

Scan the same code again → **Checked out at …**.

### A new person wants to join

1. **Students** tab → **Register a student**.
2. **Full name** and **Date of birth** are enough. Add phone, email, area (not the full address),
   level, and a mentor if you know.
3. **Under 18?** The parent must be there:
   - parent's name and phone, relation;
   - look at the parent's ID and tick **which** ID you saw — **do not write the number**;
   - tick photo consent **only if** the parent agrees;
   - the parent fills in and **signs the paper consent form**; keep it in the class file; tick that box.
4. **Save student** → note the **roll number** for them.
5. Tell them to install the app and sign up with **the same email**.

### Closing time (20:00)

**Here now** → **Check out all** → **Yes, check out all**.

### Someone has stopped coming

**Calls** tab → tap the name → **Call** → after the call choose what happened (**Coming back**,
**Taking a break**, **Not reachable**, **Stopped coming**), a reason, and write **What was said**
(short; no medical details). **Save call**.

### A student showed a lesson today

Open the student → **Tick syllabus items** → tap the box. Add a remark if useful.

### Something goes wrong

- *"Code not recognised"* → search the name instead.
- *"Waiting for access"* on a student's phone → their app email is different from the one on their
  record; tell the Facilitator.
- Anything else → note the **Version** line at the bottom of the home screen and tell the Facilitator.

### Never

- Never write an ID number, a full address or health details.
- Never share a student's phone number or photo outside the class.
- Never post a student's photo without the parent's photo consent.
