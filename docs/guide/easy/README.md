Hare Krishna. 🙏

This is the **easy guide**. It is for everyone who is **not** a computer person:

- **school children** who come to learn the mridanga, and their parents,
- **college students**,
- **doctors, nurses and others from the medical field** who serve the class as parents,
  coordinators or volunteers.

You do not need to know anything about apps or computers. Every step is written in everyday words.

## The app in three lines

1. **It is our class register on the phone.** Who came, when, and what they have learnt.
2. **It replaces our many WhatsApp groups.** Class news, lessons and events are in one place.
3. **It is made as seva** (service to Krishna). It is free, and it keeps everyone's details safe.

## Words you will see

| Word | Simple meaning |
|---|---|
| **Mridanga** (also **khol**) | The clay or fibreglass drum we play in kirtan |
| **Kirtan** | Singing the holy names together |
| **Seva** | Service done with love, without payment |
| **Facilitator** | The Guru who leads the class (the app says "Facilitator" in English) |
| **Coordinator** | A senior devotee who teaches and runs the class each day |
| **Roll number** | Your class ID, like `MS-2026-0001`. It is yours for life |
| **QR code** | The square black-and-white picture on your phone that the coordinator scans when you arrive |
| **Level** | Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced |
| **Bol** | The name of one drum stroke, such as *tā* or *dhin* |
| **Taal** | A rhythm cycle, such as the 8-beat Kaherva |

More words: [glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md).

## If you get stuck

- Ask any **coordinator** at the class. They are happy to help.
- Show them the small line at the very bottom of your home screen (it starts with *Version*). It
  tells them which version of the app you have.
- You cannot break the app by tapping around. Anything that deletes or changes something important
  asks you first ("Are you sure?"). Try, and ask if unsure.
