# For college students

Hare Krishna! 🙏 This page is for college students who learn at the class. It is short and
practical: what to do on day one, every visit, at home, and in exam season.

## How the class works for a busy student

- The class is open **every day from 14:30 to 20:00**. There are no fixed batches. Come when your
  timetable allows, and stay as long as you can.
- You learn at your own pace through three levels: **Beginner**, **Intermediate**, **Advanced**.
- One coordinator is your **mentor**. If you stop coming for a while, they will phone you to ask
  how you are. That is care, not a complaint.

## Day one (about 10 minutes)

1. At the desk, a coordinator registers you. It takes two minutes. Give your **name**, **date of
   birth** and the **email** you will use for the app. Only your area and pincode are asked, not
   your full address.
2. You get a **roll number** such as `MS-2026-0030`. It is yours for life, even if you take a long
   break.
3. Install the app: on **Android** from the link the class shares; on an **iPhone**, open the class
   web address in Safari → **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Create an account**: your name, the same email, a password of 8 or more characters. Open
   the confirmation email and tap its link. Then **Sign in**.

If you are under 18, a parent must sign the consent form first (see
[For school students](for-school-students.md#for-parents)).

## Every visit

- Open the app → **My QR card** → the coordinator scans it when you come and when you leave.
- It works without internet, so a weak signal in the temple hall is fine.
- Forgot your phone? Tell the coordinator your name; they mark you by hand.

## What is on your home screen

| You see | It means |
|---|---|
| **Visits this week** | How often you came this week |
| **Days since your last visit** | A gentle reminder 🙂 |
| **My level** with a bar | How much of your level's syllabus is ticked |
| The ring of round buttons | **My QR**, **Announcements**, **My progress**, **Assessments**, **Practice**, **Attendance**, **My profile**, **Events & polls** |
| The bell | New notifications |

Tabs at the bottom: **Home**, **My QR**, **News**, **Slokas**.

## Learn and practise

- **My progress**: every item of your level, in order. Your coordinator ticks an item when you
  play it in class. Under each item are its lessons: videos, notation PDFs, photos.
- **Lesson videos**: tap **Watch**. Slow it to **0.5x**, use **− 5 s** to go back, and **Repeat a part
  (A-B loop)** to loop a hard phrase. On the class's own videos, **Mirrored** shows the teacher's
  hands as if you were playing.
- **Practice**: a **Metronome** (tap the tempo, choose beats in a bar), a **Taal player** that loops a
  rhythm at your speed, and **Both heads**, which lights up the part of each drum head for every
  stroke.
- **Practice timer** and **My practice log**: your minutes, week by week. Your mentor sees them too,
  so they can guide you.
- **Record myself**: record with the metronome or taal playing, then listen side by side. The
  recordings stay on your phone.

## Assessments and moving up

- An **assessment** is a piece the Guru sets. Your coordinator gives it to you with a due date.
- Record it in the app (**Record here**), or choose a video from your phone, or paste a link, then
  **Send**.
- You get a score for each part (for example rhythm, clear bols, posture) and a comment, sometimes a
  voice note. **Please record it again** means one more try.
- **Moving up a level**: when you have learnt your level and come regularly, your mentor can
  nominate you. Other coordinators give their view. **Only the Guru decides.** You get a notice when
  you are promoted.

## Events, polls and slokas

- **Events & polls**: festivals, yatras and programmes. Answer **Going**, **Maybe** or **Not going**,
  and **Add to calendar** so you do not forget. If you are chosen to perform, you see **Your part**.
- **Polls**: tap your answer. Some polls are **Anonymous**: nobody, not even the Guru, sees what you chose.
- **Slokas**: a verse each day with its meaning, a recitation to listen to, and your private notes.

## Exam season, travel, a break

Life happens. If you will be away:

- Tell your mentor before you go, or answer when they call. They record **Taking a break** with a
  date, and nobody calls you again until then.
- When you come back, just come. Your first visit makes you **Active** again. Nothing is lost:
  your roll number, ticks and history stay.

## Your privacy

- Other students cannot see your record, your phone number or your marks.
- Coordinators and the Guru see what they need to teach and to care for you.
- A reply to an announcement goes only to the person who posted it and the Guru.
- On a shared or hostel phone, tap **Sign out** when you finish.
- Never share your password or a photo of your QR card.

## Want to give back?

College students are some of our best helpers:

- **Test** new parts of the app on the test version and tell us what confused you.
- **Translate**: if your Telugu or Hindi is good, help check the app's words.
- **Study computer science or engineering?** The app's code is open. Start here:
  [For a new helper](../08-new-helper.md).
- With time and practice, you may also serve as a **coordinator**. Ask the Guru.
