# For school students

Hare Krishna! 🙏 Welcome to the mridanga class.

This page tells you how to use the class app, step by step. If a word is new, ask your parent or a
coordinator. There is a part for **parents** at the end.

## Think of it like your school

The app does the same jobs your school does, but for the mridanga class:

| At school you have… | In the app you have… |
|---|---|
| A school **ID card** | **My QR** — a square code on your phone. Show it when you come and when you go |
| The teacher's **attendance register** | Your **visits**: the app writes down each time you came |
| A **report card** | **My progress** — the drum lessons you have learnt, ticked by your teacher |
| The **notice board** | **News** — messages from the class |
| **Homework** | **Assessments** — a piece to play at home, record and send |
| **Annual day** and functions | **Events** — festivals and programmes where the class plays |
| A **class vote** ("which day for the picnic?") | **Polls** — tap your answer |
| A **practice notebook** | **Practice** — a beat-keeper, the drum rhythms, and a timer for your practice |

## Before you start

1. **Come to the class with your mother, father or guardian** the first time.
2. Your parent fills in and **signs a paper form** (a "consent form") at the desk. This is the rule
   for everyone under 18. It keeps you safe.
3. The coordinator puts your name in the class records. You get a **roll number**, like
   `MS-2026-0012`.
4. Give the coordinator the **email** you will use for the app. It can be your parent's email.

You can come to class even without the app. The coordinator will find your name and mark you.

## Get the app

- **Android phone:** the class sends a link. Open it, download the app, and install it. Ask your
  parent to help the first time. ([Pictures of the steps are in the full guide](../05-phones-and-updates.md#android-install-from-a-link).)
- **iPhone:** open the class web address in **Safari**, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

## Make your account (5 minutes)

1. Open the app. Tap **Create an account**.
2. Type your **Full name**, the **Email** you gave the coordinator, and a **Password** (8 letters or
   more). Type the password again.
3. Tap **Create account**.
4. Open the email the class sent you, and tap the link inside. This proves the email is yours.
5. Go back to the app and tap **Sign in**.

If the app says **Waiting for access**, tell a coordinator. They will connect your account. Then tap
**Check again**.

## Every class day

**When you arrive:**

1. Open the app and tap the big **My QR card** button.
2. Show your phone to the coordinator. They scan it. Done — you are "checked in".

**When you leave:** show it again. They scan it again.

**No internet?** No problem. Your QR card still works without internet.

## Look at your progress

Tap **My progress**. You see all the things to learn at your level, one by one.

- A **tick** ✓ means you showed it in class and your teacher ticked it, with the date.
- **Not yet** means it is still to learn.
- Some lessons have **videos**. Tap **Watch**. You can slow the video down, and repeat a hard part
  again and again.

Moving up to the next level is decided by the Guru, after asking the coordinators who teach you.

## Practise at home

Tap **Practice**.

- **Metronome**: a steady "tick, tick" to keep time. Make it slow at first. Go faster when it is easy.
- **Taal player**: plays a rhythm in a loop so you can play along. **Speed 50 %** makes it slow.
- **Both heads**: a drawing of the drum. When a stroke plays, the part of the drum you should hit
  lights up. Left hand on the big head (baya), right hand on the small head (dayan).
- **Practice timer**: starts when you press **Start**. When you finish, tap **Stop and save**. Your
  practice minutes are saved, week by week.
- **Record myself**: record your playing and listen to it. It stays on your phone only.

Tip: 15 minutes every day is better than 2 hours once a week. 🥁

## Homework from the class (assessments)

Sometimes the class gives you a piece to play at home and send.

1. Tap **Assessments**. Open the one with the nearest date.
2. Read what to play.
3. Tap **Record here**, play, then **Stop**. Listen to it. If it is good, tap **Use for this assessment**.
4. Tap **Send**.
5. Later, your coordinator sends back marks and a comment. If they say **Please record it again**,
   practise and send it again. Everyone has to try again sometimes. That is how we learn.

## News, events and polls

- **News**: messages from the class, like "No class on Friday". **New** means you have not read it.
- **Events & polls**: festivals and programmes. Tap **Going**, **Maybe** or **Not going**. For a poll,
  tap your answer. You can change it until it closes.
- The **bell** at the top shows how many new messages you have.

## Slokas

The **Slokas** tab has a Sanskrit verse each day (**sloka of the day**), with its meaning in your
language. You can listen to it, tick **I have memorised this sloka**, and write your own notes. Only
you can read your notes.

## Stay safe — 5 golden rules

1. **Never share your password**, not even with friends.
2. **Do not send a photo of your QR card** to anyone. It is only for you.
3. Use the app **only for class**. Nobody from the class will ever ask you for money or secrets
   through the app.
4. If anything in the app worries you, **tell your parent or the Guru**.
5. When you finish on a shared phone, tap **Sign out** (at the bottom of the home screen).

## For parents

- **Why the consent form?** Indian law (the DPDP Act) asks for a parent's permission before a
  child's details are kept. The app **will not save** a child under 18 without it — the system
  itself refuses, so nobody can skip it by mistake.
- **What is kept about your child?** Name, date of birth, phone and email if given, the **area and
  pincode** only (not your full address), level, visits, and lessons learnt. Your details as
  parent. For your ID, only the **type** of ID the coordinator saw (for example "Aadhaar") — never
  the number.
- **Who can see it?** Only the coordinators and the Guru. Other students cannot see your child's
  record. Your child sees only their own.
- **Photos.** A photo of your child is used only if you ticked "photo consent" on the form.
- **Changing your mind.** You may withdraw consent at any time. Speak to the Guru.
- **Phone and email.** Coordinators may call you if your child stops coming, to ask if all is well.
- It costs nothing. There are no payments in the app.
