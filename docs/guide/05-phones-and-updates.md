# 5. Phones, install and updates

How to get the app on an Android phone, an iPhone or a laptop, how it updates itself, and how to
choose the language.

## Which version do I use?

| You have | Use | Notifications |
|---|---|---|
| An Android phone | The **Android app**, installed from a link | Yes |
| An iPhone | The **web version** in Safari, added to the home screen | Not yet |
| A laptop or desktop | The **web version** in any browser | No |

All three are the same app, made from the same code, with the same screens and rules (see
[How the app works](06-how-it-works.md#one-code-three-kinds-of-device)).

There are two copies of everything: a **test** copy with made-up students, for practice and
demos, and the **live** copy the class uses. They look the same. The test web site is
`https://mridanga-seva-test.pages.dev`; the live one, from the pilot on, is
`https://mridanga-seva.pages.dev`. Use the one the class tells you to.

## Android: install from a link

The Android app is not on the Play Store yet. It is shared as a link to an **APK** file (an
Android app package) ([DECISIONS.md #24](../DECISIONS.md)).

1. Open the link the class shares, **on the phone**.
2. Download the APK.
3. When Android asks, allow your browser to **install unknown apps**.
4. Install. Android may warn that the app is from an unknown developer; choose to install anyway.
5. Open **Mridanga Seva**, sign in, and allow notifications when asked.

A newer APK installs over the old one and keeps your login. A phone holds either the test app or
the live app, not both: installing one replaces the other, and you sign in again.

## iPhone: Safari and Add to Home Screen

1. Open the web address in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share** (the square with an arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open **Mridanga Seva** from the home screen. It opens full screen with its own icon, like an
   installed app.
4. Sign in **inside the home-screen app**. It keeps its own login, apart from Safari.

Two things to know:

- Links in emails (confirm your email, reset your password) open in **Safari**, not in the
  home-screen app. Do the step there, then go back to the home-screen app and sign in.
- The iPhone version has **no notifications** yet. Open the app to see new announcements.

Scanning QR codes also works in the web version on an iPhone (for coordinators). The camera reader
is downloaded once from a public code library; camera pictures never leave the phone. If a
network blocks that download, search the student's name instead.

## Laptop or desktop

Open the web address in a browser and sign in. On a wide screen the staff tabs move to a sidebar
on the left, and long lists show two columns. The sign-in screens show as a card in the middle of
the page.

## Updates on Android

The Android app updates itself, without a new install, for most changes
([DECISIONS.md #35](../DECISIONS.md)).

1. When the app starts, or comes back to the front (at most every 30 minutes), it asks for a newer
   version and downloads it in the background.
2. When one is ready, the home screen shows **A new version is ready**: *Restart the app to use it.
   It takes a few seconds.*
3. Tap **Restart now**. If you do not, the new version starts the next time the app is opened
   from scratch.
4. If the app cannot restart by itself, it says so: close the app fully (swipe it away) and open it
   again.

At the very bottom of the home screen, under **Sign out**, a line says which version you have:

- *Version 1.0.0 · as installed, …* (the APK as it was installed), or
- *Version 1.0.0 · update of 01-10-2026 15:30 (a1b2c3d4)* (an update, with its date and code).

**Please quote this line when you report a problem.**

Some changes cannot come as an update and need a new APK, for example a new camera or audio
feature. The class will share a new link when that happens. Why? See
[How an update reaches phones](06-how-it-works.md#how-an-update-reaches-phones).

The web version (iPhone, laptop) is updated on the server, so it always shows the newest version
the next time it is opened.

## Choosing your language

The app speaks **English**, **తెలుగు** (Telugu) and **हिन्दी** (Hindi).

- Change it on the sign-in screen, at the foot of the home screen, or on **My profile**.
- Language names are always written in their own script, so you can find yours.
- Your choice is saved on your profile, so a new phone opens in your language after you sign in.
- With no choice made, the app follows the phone's language if it is Telugu or Hindi, and English
  otherwise.

The Telugu and Hindi texts are drafts made with care but not yet checked by native speakers. If a
word reads oddly or is cut off, tell a coordinator, with a screenshot if you can. How translation
works: [TRANSLATIONS.md](../TRANSLATIONS.md).

## Without internet

- **My QR card** shows the copy saved on the phone and can still be scanned.
- If you were signed in before, the app still opens on your home screen from what it saved last
  time ([DECISIONS.md #37, #42](../DECISIONS.md)). New numbers and announcements load when the
  connection returns.
- Marking attendance, saving calls and posting announcements need the internet, because they are
  saved in the class records on the server.
