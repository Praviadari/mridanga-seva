Help us test check-in with your face

Before we offer face check-in to anyone, we want to try it with a few adult volunteers of our class. You check in and out of class by looking at your own phone, instead of showing your QR code.

Who can help

  • Aged 18 or over (no children in this test)
  • Have an Android phone (iPhones are not part of this test)
  • Can come to class on the test days: {{DATES}}

What you will do

  • Install a separate test app, "Mridanga Seva TEST". Your normal app stays as it is.
  • Read and sign a short consent form at the desk, then set up your face once (about 5 minutes).
  • Check in and out with your face a few times, and help us try to fool it (a photo, someone else's face).

Your face and your privacy

  • No photo is kept. Your face code stays only on your phone; it is compared only with you.
  • Your location is read only at the moment you check in or out, never tracked.
  • Everything is deleted within 7 days after the test. You can stop at any time.
  • Saying no changes nothing: your QR code keeps working as always.
